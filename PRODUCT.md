# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Families — parents, grandparents, teachers — and their young children, mostly NZ/Pacific plus a global YouTube audience. Parents buy songs, albums and videos; kids experience the content (and seasonal features like the Santa tracker). Used at home and in classrooms, often on mid-range phones and tablets.

## Product Purpose

lovetosing.com is the Shopify storefront for Love to Sing, a children's music brand (Christmas songs, celebration songs, sing-alongs). It sells digital songs, albums and video downloads, and hosts seasonal experiences (Christmas countdown / Santa tracker) that drive annual traffic and funnel visitors to the song catalogue.

## Brand Personality

Joyful, warm, family-first. Happy kids' music, crafted rather than childish — warm reds and golds, hearts and music notes. Parents trust it; kids love it. Magic over realism in seasonal features.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS aesthetics: stock palettes (coral/mint/sky), gradient text, cookie-cutter glass cards, hero-metric templates.
- Clip-art kiddie clutter: cheap clip-art, rainbow overload, Comic Sans energy.
- Cold corporate minimalism: sterile grays, sharp edges, no warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth carries the brand** — brand red (`--c-red-*`) and gold (`--c-yellow-*`) do the emotional work; navy (`--c-blue-*`) sets the night-time stage for seasonal magic.
2. **Crafted, not childish** — playful moments (snow, hearts, sleigh) sit on disciplined typography and spacing; delight is earned, never noisy.
3. **Use the theme's tokens** — colors, spacing (`--space-*`), radii (`--radius-*`) and type (`--fz-*`, heading font) come from `assets/base.css` / theme settings; no off-palette values.
4. **A 6-year-old on a cheap Android is the floor** — performance, clarity and touch targets before decoration.
5. **Every idle moment nudges gently toward the songs** — CTAs to the catalogue are present but never shouty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation (seasonal features are motion-heavy). Keyboard access and visible focus for all interactive elements. Child-friendly touch targets (≥44px).
